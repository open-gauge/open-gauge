title: Calibration wizard field reference
description: What each field on the calibration wizard's first step means, and an example of how to fill it.
---

A field-by-field reference for Step 1 ("General info") of the calibration wizard — what each
field is for, and a concrete example. For the wizard's overall flow, see
[Calibration overview](./overview.mdx); for the type/purpose classification these fields feed
into, see [Calibration type](./overview.mdx#calibration-type) and
[Calibration purpose](./overview.mdx#calibration-purpose).

## Channel

The sensor channel this calibration record applies to. Each channel keeps an independent
calibration history and due date — a multi-channel asset (e.g. a transmitter with separate
pressure and temperature outputs) shows this field; a single-channel asset doesn't need it, since
there's only one channel to choose. Example: a 3-channel data logger shows "CH2 — Humidity" here
when you're calibrating its humidity input specifically.

## Calibration date

The date the reference/measured comparison was actually performed — not the date you're entering
the record. This is used to compute the due date from the calibration interval below. Example: a
calibration performed in the lab on `2026-03-14` still gets recorded with that date even if you
enter it into Open Gauge a week later.

## Registered by

Who performed and is recording this calibration — defaults to you, editable to credit another
team member who actually did the work. Example: you're entering historical data on behalf of a
colleague who performed the calibration last month — pick their name here instead of your own.

## Checked by

An optional second reviewer. Assigning one puts the record into **pending approval** instead of
valid until they approve or reject it — see
[Approval workflow](./overview.mdx#approval-workflow). Leave it unset for calibrations that don't
need a second sign-off; the record is valid immediately.

## Calibration interval

Months until this channel's next calibration is due, e.g. `12` for an annual cadence or `6` for
semi-annual. Pre-filled from the channel's configured default interval, but editable per
calibration — a channel drifting faster than expected might get a shorter interval this time.

## Calibration type

Who performed the calibration — **OEM**, an **External Accredited Lab**, your own **Internal Lab
(In-house)**, or a **Customer's Asset** you calibrated on their behalf. This determines what the
Calibration lab field below resolves to. See
[Calibration type](./overview.mdx#calibration-type) for the full breakdown of each option.

## Calibration purpose

Why this calibration was performed: **Initial** (the channel's first ever), **Routine** (a
periodic recalibration), **After Repair** (following a fix — reveals repair date/description
fields below), or **Verification** (a one-off check). See
[Calibration purpose](./overview.mdx#calibration-purpose) for details, including how an After
Repair record feeds the Health tab's before/after comparison.

## Calibration lab

Where — or by whom — the calibration was carried out. This field's shape changes with Calibration
type: a read-only snapshot of the asset's manufacturer for **OEM**, a picker over your external
organizations for **External Accredited Lab**/**Customer's Asset**, or a picker over your
configured locations for **Internal Lab**. Example: choosing Internal Lab shows a dropdown of
your organization's locations marked as calibration labs, e.g. "Building 3 — Metrology Lab".

## Calibration certificate

Upload the lab-issued PDF certificate — available for **OEM** and **External Accredited Lab**
types only. Open Gauge validates that the file is actually a PDF (both by declared type and file
signature) before accepting it. If provided, it's served instead of the system-generated
certificate everywhere this calibration's certificate is downloaded.

## Reference asset

The traceable reference standard used for this **Internal Lab** calibration — one of your own
assets flagged as a reference standard, e.g. a master gauge with its own valid, in-date
calibration. Open Gauge automatically pulls that reference asset's own expanded uncertainty into
this calibration's uncertainty budget once selected.

## Calibration method

The internal procedure followed for this **Internal Lab** calibration, e.g. "Pressure Comparison
Method — 0 to 100 bar". Procedures are configured per physical quantity; only ones matching the
selected channel's quantity are offered.

## Repair date

When the repair that triggered this **After Repair** calibration was completed. This date becomes
a boundary on the Health tab's before/after-repair comparison dropdown.

## Repair description

A short note (up to 500 characters) on what was repaired or replaced before this calibration.
Example: "Replaced the diaphragm and re-seated the pressure fitting after a leak was found during
routine inspection."

## Add frequency response

Record a swept frequency response — amplitude and/or phase measured across a range of
frequencies — alongside the standard reference/measured data points, for channels where dynamic
(not just static) response matters, e.g. accelerometers or acoustic sensors. See
[Frequency response](./frequency-response.mdx) for the full sweep-entry flow this unlocks.

## Environmental conditions

Temperature, pressure, and humidity at the time of calibration, each with its own optional
uncertainty (±) — recorded for traceability on the certificate, not folded into the fit or
uncertainty budget. Example: "23.1 ± 0.2 °C" records both the reading and how confident you are
in that reading, e.g. from the lab thermometer's own calibration certificate.

## Notes

Free-text notes attached to this calibration record — anything worth remembering that doesn't fit
elsewhere, e.g. an observation about the asset's condition or an anomaly seen during the
comparison.
